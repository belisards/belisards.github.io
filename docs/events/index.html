Organization

Organized several editions of the Brazilian Conference on Data Journalism and Digital Methods (Coda.Br) from 2018 to 2022, including the first regional meeting in the Amazon region. Collaborated with Witness to organize the first national meeting on deepfakes and developed a series of events about free culture and piracy (Copyfight).
