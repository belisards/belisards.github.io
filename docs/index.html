Adriano Belisario

Data journalist and researcher combining expertise in communication, open‑source technologies, and data to address human rights concerns. Currently at the Joint Data Center on Forced Displacement, a collaboration between the World Bank and UNHCR. Previously contributed to Forensic Architecture, Agência Pública, The Intercept Brasil, and Open Knowledge Brazil. Holds an MSc in Social Data Science from the Oxford Internet Institute.
